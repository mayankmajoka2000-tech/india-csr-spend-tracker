The CSR rule

  • Surplus from CSR activities cannot become company profit. It must go back into the project, an Unspent CSR Account or a Schedule VII fund (CSR Rules 2014, Rule 7(2) as amended 2021; MCA FAQ 55). So in this model credit revenue goes to farmers and the project's own costs, and any leftover is shown as surplus to plough back.
  • Whether a company may count CSR-funded credits toward its own targets has not been clarified by MCA. Take legal advice before structuring one.
  • Verra cancelled 37 legacy rice projects (4.5 million credits) over additionality. Projects in areas that already drain fields face that risk.
